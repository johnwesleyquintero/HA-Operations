## Case Study Standards

For each case study, capture:

1. **Background** — Brand/team context and what was happening before the work.
2. **Problem** — The operational or commercial bottleneck.
3. **Objective** — What needed to improve, including constraints.
4. **Investigation** — Reports, data, customer signals, or workflow review used.
5. **Approach** — Decisions and why they were chosen.
6. **Execution** — What Wesley personally did and what others owned.
7. **Outcome** — Verified result, directional observation, or unresolved measurement.
8. **Current status** — Whether the work is ongoing, completed, reused, or being monitored.
9. **Evidence to collect** — Reports, before/after comparisons, or records needed to strengthen the story.
10. **Portfolio boundary** — What must be generalized or excluded from public presentation.

### Accuracy Rules

- Separate Wesley's direct contribution from team/client actions.

- If a result is remembered but not yet supported by a retrievable report, label it as unquantified or pending validation.

## Signpactive — PPC Efficiency, Organic Rank Support & Listing Content

**Resume STAR ID:** `signpactive-expansion-readiness`  
**Related tool STAR:** `wesbid-signpactive-build`  
**Current resume title:** PPC & SEO Optimization with WesBid (Signpactive)  
**Category:** Advertising / SEO / Listing Optimization

### Background

Signpactive's Amazon growth work involved managing paid targeting while protecting the organic visibility and Best Sellers Rank (BSR) supported by campaign performance. PPC efficiency could not be considered in isolation: reducing spend indiscriminately could also weaken productive traffic and ranking support. Listing content was another part of the overall search and conversion experience.

### Problem

Campaign targeting needed a clearer way to distinguish productive targets from spend-heavy underperformers. Reviews also needed to be organized around shopper intent so optimization decisions could be made with more context than a simple bid increase/decrease. At the listing level, SEO and A+ Content needed attention alongside PPC.

### Objective

- Reduce wasted advertising spend and improve ACoS.
- Identify underperforming targets ("bleeders") for review.
- Group targets by shopper/search intent to support more deliberate decisions.
- Retain a strong BSR while improving PPC efficiency.
- Align listing SEO and A+ Content with the broader search and shopper experience.
- Keep ongoing campaign decisions within the client's budget.

### Approach & Execution

1. **WesBid analysis:** Developed WesBid to analyze Amazon PPC targeting performance and make spend-heavy underperformers easier to identify.
2. **Bleeder identification:** Used the analysis to surface targets consuming spend without sufficient performance, so they could be reviewed rather than treated as undifferentiated campaign data.
3. **Intent clustering:** Organized targets into intent-based clusters to make optimization decisions more relevant to the shopper query and target purpose.
4. **PPC and organic balance:** Evaluated efficiency with organic visibility and BSR in mind, rather than treating ACoS as the only success measure.
5. **SEO support:** Worked on listing SEO as part of the broader search visibility effort.
6. **A+ Content normalization:** Standardized A+ Content to create a more consistent listing content experience.
7. **Ongoing management:** Campaigns are currently monitored, and adjustments are made in line with the client's available budget and priorities.

### Outcome

ACoS was reduced while maintaining a strong BSR. The work combined PPC analysis and intent-based targeting decisions with SEO improvements and normalized A+ Content.

The current operating state is campaign monitoring with budget-aligned adjustments. No percentage, revenue lift, or isolated causal contribution is stated here; add quantified results only when the relevant campaign and ranking evidence is available.

### Evidence to Add Later

- Before/after campaign-period ACoS and spend, with comparable date ranges.
- Targeting/search-term report examples showing bleeder identification and intent clustering.
- BSR history for the relevant products and period.
- Listing SEO change log or keyword/indexing evidence.
- A+ Content before/after captures or change notes.
- Campaign budget and change history to show how ongoing adjustments followed client constraints.

### Portfolio Notes

- Describe WesBid as originally developed primarily for the Signpactive PPC workflow.
- Keep the separate `wesbid-signpactive-build` STAR focused on tool development and repeatable PPC decision support.
- Keep this STAR focused on the business application: PPC efficiency, intent clustering, organic rank support, SEO, and A+ Content.
- Do not claim that WesBid alone caused every performance outcome. The work included PPC and listing-content changes.
- Do not publish client-specific campaign data, ASINs, search-term exports, budgets, or account screenshots without authorization.
