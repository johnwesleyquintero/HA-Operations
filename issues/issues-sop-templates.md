### Inventory Discrepancy

**Subject**
```
Investigate the Inventory Discrepancy in Shipment ID:
```

**Message**
```
XH-0GWW-H0T3, -12 Please investigate.



Hello,

We are writing regarding the recent merging of our inquiries for this shipment.

We ask that you please stop merging our follow-up cases. Merging inquiries often prevents a proper, dedicated investigation and delays the resolution of our missing inventory claims. We are still waiting for a proper review of the documents we have already provided.

The denial of our reimbursement based on "unexpected units" is unacceptable and does not explain the whereabouts of our missing goods. Our inbound shipment was perfectly aligned with the shipping plan. Box contents, tracking, SKUs, and UPCs were all accurate. The fulfillment center's claim of receiving unexpected items does not excuse missing inventory; those units should have simply been categorized as overages.

We have already submitted all required documents confirming that the shipped items and quantities matched the shipment plan exactly. These documents include all relevant shipping records and proof of delivery, which clearly demonstrate that the missing units were included in the original shipment.

We expect Amazon to take full accountability for inventory lost while in your care. Please reopen this case, escalate it to the appropriate department, and process our reimbursement promptly.

Thank you.


Wesley
Attachments:
FBA19BV0GF17.pdf
```
