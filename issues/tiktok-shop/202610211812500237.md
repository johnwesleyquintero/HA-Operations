## Issue and Resolution

**Issue:** Confirm the correct appointment-booking procedure for TikTok Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT) LTL shipments handled by Estes.

**TikTok Support guidance:**

- When Estes is responsible for booking the delivery appointment, select **“My carrier will book the delivery appointment.”**
- The seller does not need to take an additional appointment-booking action in Seller Center while the carrier is handling scheduling.
- The “Missing appointment” alert may be disregarded while the carrier is handling scheduling, according to the support guidance.
- Enter correct shipment tracking information and provide the carrier with the required inbound order details.
- Submit appointment requests at least **3 business days in advance**.
- Confirm with the carrier that the appointment is booked before delivery.

**Scope:** PO reference AMC0015150 — Estes.
