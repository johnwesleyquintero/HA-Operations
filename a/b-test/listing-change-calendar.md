## Operating rules

1. **One listing change per product at a time.** Do not run overlapping listing changes on the same product; resolve overlap flags before launch.
2. **Price or coupon changes must never overlap listing changes.** Coordinate with the Promo & Pricing Tracker and confirm clean measurement windows before scheduling.
3. **Keep A/B tests, seasonal rotations, and update projects distinct.** Use the correct Type value in the spreadsheet.
4. **Compact is an update project, not an A/B test.** Read performance before and after the A+ update because traffic is currently too low for a reliable experiment.
5. **Do not launch asset-dependent seasonal changes until the required creative exists and is approved.** In particular, November mashed-potato images are blocked until creation is complete.
6. **Preserve the calculated controls.** Flags are meant to expose overlap and calendar conflicts; do not treat a proposed date as approved just because it appears in the calendar.
7. **Capture evidence and learning.** Add source, baseline/result values, lift, decision, what was learned, and a proof link when available.
