# HUNGRY ARTISAN — Operations Meeting Reference:

**Weekly Agenda — Thursdays, 10:00–11:00 AM EST**  
*“From Scratch. On Purpose.”*

## Meeting Details

- **Cadence:** Weekly — every Thursday, 10:00–11:00 AM EST
- **Meeting owner and facilitator:** Wesley Quintero (Operations)
- **Reporting to:** Victoria Marino (GM) and Justin Hopkins (CEO)
- **Attendees:** Wesley Quintero, Victoria Marino, Justin Hopkins
- **Duration:** 60 minutes

## Purpose

Keep operations healthy and ahead of problems. Wesley owns and runs the meeting, presenting inventory, shipping, account and channel health, listing and A/B test updates, and the decisions needed from Victoria and Justin. Every meeting should end with clear decisions, named owners, and due dates.

## Pre-Work — Due Wednesday EOD

*Complete before the meeting; do not spend meeting time reading through the preparation.*

- Wesley updates the Inventory / Shipping Tracker and A/B test log.
- Review account health, open cases, replenishment needs, and upcoming shipment milestones.
- Prepare a concise list of decisions and blockers requiring leadership input.

## Agenda

### 1. Inventory & Supply — 15 minutes

- FBA and AWD inventory by product and marketplace; stockout and overstock risks over the next 60–90 days.
- Inbound pipeline from the Shipping Tracker: PI and balance payments due, inspections, ETD/ETA, and arrivals within 14 days.
- Reorder forecast and purchase orders requiring a decision.
- AWD → FBA, Walmart, and TikTok replenishment needs.

### 2. Account & Channel Health — 10 minutes

- Amazon account and listing health, Buy Box, and open cases.
- Brand Registry and intellectual property issues.
- Channel status: Amazon CA / UK, Walmart, TikTok Shop, and Shopify.

### 3. Listing Updates & A/B Tests — 10 minutes

- Tests running and completed; results requiring a decision.
- Pending listing, image, and A+ updates, including image swaps timed to old-stock runway.
- Creative or asset requests that are blocked.

### 4. Logistics & 3PL — 5 minutes

- Proline (Canada) and SSD Logistix (UK) replenishment status.
- Shipment discrepancies, check-in issues, and lost, damaged, stranded, or delayed inventory.

### 5. Decisions & Blockers — 10 minutes

- Decisions needed from Victoria or Justin: PO approvals, reorder quantities, pricing, and listing changes.
- Cross-functional needs involving PPC (Mae), launches (Maria), and content (Juls).

### 6. Next Week Commitments — 5 minutes

- Wesley’s priorities, with owners and due dates.
- Items to flag in Monday’s operations report to Victoria and in the L10.

### 7. Q4 Rocks Check-In — R6 and R1 / R2 Support — 5 minutes

- **R6 — Logistics optimization (Wesley):** October 31 — multi-forwarder quoting live (AGL plus two alternatives for each China–US / Canada / UK shipment); November 30 — total-cost analysis and written recommendation to Justin; December 31 — first shipment under the new framework with a documented cost comparison.
- **R2 support:** Wesley runs A/B test execution across all five ASINs. Classic Food Mill CTR target: **≥1.2% by November 15**, up from 0.96%.
- **R1 support:** Flag any stockout or inbound delay that puts Q4 channel revenue at risk. UK revenue is $0 in the current forecast and needs visibility.
- Record each rock as **On Track / At Risk / Off Track**, including what is needed from Victoria or Justin.

## Standing Rules

- Wesley owns the meeting and the operational numbers.
- Read data directly from the Inventory / Shipping Tracker and Seller Central on screen rather than relying on verbal recollection.
- Pre-work is due Wednesday EOD.
- Escalate urgent issues—such as stockout risk or an account-health threat—the same day; do not hold them for Thursday.
- Victoria and Justin make purchase and pricing decisions; Wesley brings the data and a recommendation.
- Record every decision with an owner and due date.
- Continue the Monday operations report to Victoria as usual.
