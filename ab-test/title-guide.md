# Amazon Product Title Requirements and Guidelines

## Overview

The product title or product name (the **Item name** field on the List Your Products page) is the first piece of information customers see when they search for a product on Amazon.

Titles must clearly and concisely describe key product information. Clear titles help customers identify, differentiate, and compare products, simplifying decision-making and increasing the likelihood of a sale.

## 1. Product Title Requirements

Title requirements apply to all product types except media product types, across all Amazon stores except Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Türkiye, and the United Arab Emirates.

To list a product for the first time, its title must meet the following requirements:

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Character limit** | Titles must not exceed **75 characters**, including spaces. |
| **Promotional content** | Titles must not contain promotional phrases such as "free shipping" or "100% quality guaranteed." |
| **Special characters** | The following characters are prohibited in titles: `!`, `$`, `?`, `_`, `{`, `}`, `^`, `¬`, `¦`. Other special characters, such as `~`, `#`, `<`, `>`, and `*`, are allowed only in specific contexts, such as product identifiers or measurements. Decorative use is not allowed. |
| **Content requirements** | Titles must contain enough information to clearly describe the product, such as "Amazon Essentials Dress," "Columbia Hiking Boots," or "Sony Headphones." Restricted phrases, such as "FSA/HSA eligible," must not be included. |
| **Word repetition** | The same word must not appear more than twice. Prepositions, articles, and conjunctions may appear more than twice. Brand names are also subject to the two-instance limit. |

### Existing Listings

If an existing product title does not comply with these requirements or contains inaccurate information, Amazon may automatically correct it or prevent it from appearing in search results.

Policy-violating titles can be reviewed in:
- **Manage All Inventory**
- **Review listing changes** for brand owners

After submitting title or item highlights changes, allow **24–48 hours** for updates to be processed and displayed in Amazon stores.

## 2. Best Practices

High-quality product titles help customers discover products and can improve conversions. Customers scan search results quickly, so titles should prioritize important product information without excessive detail or unnecessary wording.

If the title cannot accommodate all relevant product information within the character limit, use the **Item highlights** field to provide additional descriptive information.

### Title Guidelines

| Guideline | Compliant Example | Non-Compliant Example | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Length** | `Amazon Essentials Women's Fleece Jacket, Black, Medium` | `Amazon Essentials Super Comfortable Women's Winter Fleece Jacket with Pockets and Zipper Closure Perfect for Cold Weather and Outdoor Activities, Available in Black Color, Size Medium for Women` | Keep titles within 75 characters because mobile screens truncate long titles. |
| **Redundancy** | `Levi's Men's 501 Original Fit Jeans` | `Levi's Men's Jeans Men's 501 Original Fit Men's Denim Jeans` | Avoid repeating words and information unnecessarily. |
| **Special characters** | `Brother Printer, HL-L2350DW Wireless Monochrome` | `Brother Printer★HL-L2350DW★Wireless Monochrome★BEST SELLER★` | Avoid excessive or decorative symbols. Use necessary punctuation and standard characters. |
| **Information order** | `Amazon Fresh Decaf Colombia Whole Bean Coffee, Medium Roast, 12 Ounce` | `Medium Roast Decaf Coffee 12oz Pack of 3 Colombia Whole Bean Amazon Fresh` | Prioritize important information at the beginning of the title. |
| **Parent-child variations** | Parent: `Amazon Essentials T-Shirt`<br>Child: `Amazon Essentials T-Shirt, White, Medium` | Parent: `Amazon Essentials T-Shirt, Available in Multiple Colors and Sizes`<br>Child: Same as parent | Include size and color in child ASIN titles, not in the parent title. |
| **Numbers** | `2-Pack Cotton Towels, 24 x 48 inches` | `Two-Pack Cotton Towels, Twenty-Four x Forty-Eight inches` | Use numerals instead of spelling out numbers. |
| **Subjective commentary** | `Sony WH-1000XM4 Wireless Noise Canceling Headphones` | `Sony WH-1000XM4 HOT ITEM Wireless Noise Canceling Headphones - BEST SELLER!!!` | Avoid subjective promotional phrases such as "Hot Item" and "Best seller." |
| **Capitalization** | `Nike Air Running Shoes with Cushioned Sole` | `NIKE AIR RUNNING SHOES` or `nike air running shoes` | Capitalize the first letter of each word, except prepositions, conjunctions, and articles. |
| **Abbreviations** | `Samsung 55-inch 4K Smart TV, 55 in, 2023 Model` | `Samsung Fifty-Five inch 4K Smart Television, Fifty-Five inches, 2023 Model` | Measurement abbreviations such as `cm`, `oz`, `in`, and `kg` are acceptable. |
| **Model numbers** | `Samsung Galaxy S23 Ultra, 256GB, Phantom Black, SM-S918U` | `Samsung Galaxy Phone, 256GB, Phantom Black` | Include model numbers for categories where they are recommended. Refer to the category-specific style guide. |

### Additional Notes

- Do not use non-language characters such as `Æ`, `Š`, `Œ`, `Ÿ`, or `Ž`.
- Necessary punctuation, including hyphens (`-`), forward slashes (`/`), commas (`,`), ampersands (`&`), and periods (`.`), may be used.
- Special characters prohibited in titles should be entered in the **Brand name** field when they are part of the actual brand name.
- The title of the parent ASIN appears on the product detail page. The child ASIN title appears after the customer adds the child product to their cart.

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between product titles and product names?

Product titles and product names refer to the same thing on Amazon stores.

### How can I include more product details if the title limit is 75 characters?

Use the **Item highlights** field, which provides an additional **125 characters** for details such as materials and recommended use cases.

Item highlights should use comma-separated phrases rather than complete sentences.

**Example:**

Instead of a long title such as:

`Fast Charger Adapter, PPS Supported, Compact Charger for models like MacBook Air/Macbook Pro/iPhone 14/iPhone 13/Galaxy S22/iPad Pro/Pixel and More, Cable Not Included`

Use a concise title and move additional details to Item highlights:

`USB-C, PPS Support, Cable not included`

### Where do Item highlights appear?

Item highlights appear below titles in search results and on product detail pages.

### How long do title and Item highlights updates take?

After successful submission, updates are accepted immediately but may take **24–48 hours** to appear in search results and on product detail pages.

Do not resubmit the update or open a support case during this normal processing window.

If the update is still not visible after 48 hours:
1. Edit and resubmit the update, or
2. Contact Selling Partner Support.

### What if my brand name contains prohibited special characters?

Enter the brand name in the **Brand name** field. Special characters in this field are exempt from title requirements and may appear in the brand byline on detail pages and search results.

### What if the product has no brand name?

Enter `generic` in the **Brand name** field. Do not include a brand name in the title.

### How can I edit a title if I disagree with Amazon's suggested title?

Make changes in **Manage All Inventory**. Titles and Item highlights can be edited at any time.

### Should variation attributes go in Item name or Item highlights?

Include the most important variation attributes in the **Item name** field. If the 75-character limit prevents you from including all attributes, retain the most important ones in Item name and move the remaining details to Item highlights.

## 4. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| **Title suppression** | The title violates Amazon's policy requirements or contains inaccurate information. | Go to Manage All Inventory and select **Search suppressed and inactive listings**. Review the identified violations, edit the title to comply, and submit the changes. Amazon will automatically check compliance and lift suppression if the title meets requirements. |
| **Title blocked due to policy violations** | The title contains prohibited characters, promotional content, excessive repetition, or other policy violations. | Locate the blocked title in Manage All Inventory, correct the violations, ensure all title requirements are met, and submit the changes for review. |
| **Title or Item highlights not visible** | Amazon is still processing the submitted update. | Confirm the update was submitted successfully and allow 24–48 hours for processing. If the change remains invisible after 48 hours, edit and resubmit it or contact Selling Partner Support. |

### Additional Support

Amazon's Seller Assistant can provide help with product title issues and, in most language settings, can connect sellers directly to Selling Partner Support.

If the product's Browse Node is incorrect, submit a request through the **Category update tool**.

## 5. Operational Checklist

Before submitting an Amazon product title, verify the following:

- [ ] Title is **75 characters or fewer**, including spaces.
- [ ] Title clearly describes the product.
- [ ] No promotional phrases or restricted wording are included.
- [ ] No prohibited special characters are present.
- [ ] No word appears more than twice, except permitted prepositions, articles, and conjunctions.
- [ ] Brand name, product type, and important attributes appear in a logical order.
- [ ] Numbers use numerals where appropriate.
- [ ] Capitalization is consistent and readable.
- [ ] Variation attributes are placed correctly for parent and child ASINs.
- [ ] Model numbers are included where recommended for the category.
- [ ] Additional descriptive information is moved to Item highlights when necessary.
- [ ] After submission, allow 24–48 hours for the changes to appear before escalating.
