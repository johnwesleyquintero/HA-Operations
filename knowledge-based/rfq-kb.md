## 2. Standard RFQ Comparison Requirements

When collecting quotes from AGL and alternative forwarders, aim to compare equivalent services.

Capture the following where applicable:

- Forwarder name and quote date
- Origin pickup and port of loading
- Port of discharge and final destination
- Container type and quantity
- Ocean freight and origin charges
- Destination, customs, and delivery charges
- Duties and other exclusions
- Transit-time estimate and quote validity
- AWD delivery capability and appointment requirements
- Any charges or benefits affected by using a non-AGL route

**Comparison principle:** Compare the full landed logistics cost and service scope, not just the headline ocean-freight rate. Evaluate potential Amazon inbound placement fee waivers, AGL pricing advantages, and AWD auto-replenishment implications separately where relevant.
