## Data Handling

- Preserve ASINs, SKUs, FNSKUs, Walmart Item IDs, and TikTok SKU IDs exactly as text. Do not remove leading zeros or allow spreadsheet software to convert long IDs to scientific notation.
- “Not provided” and “Not confirmed” mean the value is unavailable in this reference; do not infer or fill it from another product.
- Verify identifiers against the relevant marketplace or internal tracker before making listing, replenishment, or shipment changes.
- This catalog is a reusable reference, not a substitute for the latest supplier quotation, purchase approval, or live marketplace record.
