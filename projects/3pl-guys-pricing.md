## 1. Inbound receiving

**Choose one receiving method. These fees are not combined.** The applicable charge depends on how inventory arrives at the warehouse.

| Receiving method | Rate | When it applies |
|---|---:|---|
| 20FT container (floor-loaded) | $400 per container | Container delivered directly to the warehouse |
| 40FT / 40HQ / 45FT container (floor-loaded) | $800 per container | Container delivered directly to the warehouse |
| Loose cartons / DDP shipment | $3.00 per carton | Cartons delivered loose by UPS, FedEx, freight forwarder, etc. |
| Palletized receiving | $15.00 per pallet | Product arrives already palletized; no unloading required |

### Receiving fee rules

- Only the receiving method applicable to the shipment is charged.
- A floor-loaded container is charged the container unloading fee, not an additional $3.00 per carton.
- Loose DDP cartons are charged $3.00 per carton, with no container unloading fee.
- Palletized shipments are charged $15.00 per pallet.

**Provider example:** A 40FT container with 1,000 cartons = $800 total receiving fee, not $800 + $3,000.

## 10. R6 review notes / items to clarify

This rate sheet is an initial pricing input, not a shipment-specific quote. Before calculating a comparable China-to-FBA alternative, clarify:

- Whether 3PL Guys can receive the relevant inbound freight from HA's selected China forwarder and what delivery appointments / receiving requirements apply.
- Whether container unloading rates include all unloading, count, palletization, and put-away labor, and what happens if cartons arrive floor-loaded but require palletization.
- Whether storage is prorated, how pallet counts are calculated, and whether there are minimum monthly charges or other storage-related fees.
- Whether SPD and LTL forwarding fees include only warehouse handling or also any transportation / carrier costs.
- Whether Amazon shipment labelling, carton labels, pallet labels, appointment, and carrier charges are included or billed separately.
- Whether prep service prices are additive and whether custom prep or kit assembly has separate rates.
- Whether the $29.99 monthly software fee is mandatory and whether setup, onboarding, API, or EDI fees apply.
- Whether there are minimum volume, account, order, monthly, or contract requirements.
- Whether the rates apply to the specific warehouse location and are still current.

**R6 comparison caution:** Do not treat the listed receiving, storage, prep, and forwarding fees as an all-in China-to-FBA cost. International freight, customs clearance, duties/taxes, destination transportation, Amazon charges, and other applicable costs must be identified separately and compared on equivalent shipment assumptions.
