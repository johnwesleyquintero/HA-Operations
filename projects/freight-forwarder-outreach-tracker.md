## Status definitions

- **Outreach planned:** Provider identified; no message sent yet.
- **Contacted — awaiting response:** Initial outreach sent; no substantive response received.
- **Information requested:** Provider needs verified shipment or compliance details before quoting.
- **Quote received — under review:** A quotation has arrived and needs normalization against the workbook.
- **Follow-up required:** A material gap or unanswered question remains.
- **Not pursuing / inactive:** No current action planned; retain history for reference.

## Update rules

1. Update the tracker only when a material outreach action, provider response, quote, status, or next step changes.
2. Record **Quote received = Yes** only after an actual quotation has arrived. An acknowledgment or request for more information is not a quote.
3. Keep the external request aligned to one shipment/load and its verified destination and fulfillment path. Confirm each provider's coverage; do not assume a provider serves every country, facility, or channel.
4. Compare equivalent scope. Capture the Incoterm, price basis, included/excluded charges, route, mode, transit time, quote validity, and final-mile destination. Map provider replies into the workbook; do not require providers to use our internal categories.
5. Mark unknown charges and unresolved details as pending or unclear—never assume a missing charge is zero.
6. Do not expose internal PO numbers, invoice details, internal shipment IDs, or internal cost notes in external messages. Use neutral shipment labels.
7. An RFQ is for comparison only and does not authorize a booking.
8. Review follow-up dates before acting; update stale dates based on the actual latest correspondence rather than treating an old date as a new commitment.
