## Decision

Keep the two planned Canada FBA replenishment shipments in draft and do not submit a paid capacity-increase request at this time.

- Classic Food Mill: 108 units (18 cartons at 6 units/carton)
- Premium Food Mill: 18 units (3 cartons at 6 units/carton)

The demand review showed declining projected demand into November and December, while the current Canada inventory position was assessed as sufficient to cover expected demand through January. Additional storage capacity is therefore not justified by the current replenishment outlook.

This is a deferment, not a cancellation. Reassess if actual sales accelerate, available inventory changes, or the forecast is revised.
